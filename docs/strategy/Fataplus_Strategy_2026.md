# Fataplus Strategy 2026: The Innovation Studio
## "From Agritech to High-Tech Venture Builder"

---

## 1. Executive Summary: The "Pivot Up"
**Context:**  
Fataplus spent 2023-2025 building advanced Agritech solutions (Fataplus MCP, Offline AI). While the operational side (selling produce) faced logistical hurdles, the **Technology** and **Brand Equity** achieved "Venture Grade" status.

**The Pivot:**  
We are NOT "starting over." We are **graduating**.
We are transforming from a single-product startup into a **Product Innovation Studio**.
We use the advanced methodology, tools, and AI capabilities we built for ourselves to build the next generation of Malagasy startups.

**Tagline Evolution:**
*   *Old:* "Digital Agriculture Platform"
*   *New:* **"Fataplus Studio: We Build Venture-Grade Products."**
