# Madagascar Agricultural Stakeholders Directory
## Comprehensive Analysis Report

**Publication Date:** August 5, 2025  
**Research Period:** August 2025  
**Total Stakeholders Identified:** 45 organizations  
**Data Sources:** Official websites, government portals, business directories, NGO databases, academic sources

---

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive directory maps Madagascar's agricultural ecosystem across the entire value chain, identifying 45 key stakeholders spanning government institutions, research centers, international organizations, private sector actors, NGOs, academic institutions, and emerging agritech startups. The research reveals a dynamic agricultural sector undergoing digital transformation while maintaining strong traditional foundations.

### Key Findings:
- **Government Leadership:** MINAE leads policy development with active programs like "Diaben'ny famokarana"
- **Research Excellence:** FOFIFA and CIRAD provide world-class agricultural research capabilities
- **Digital Innovation:** Emerging agritech ecosystem with $90M Israeli smart agriculture partnership
- **International Support:** Strong presence of UN agencies, development banks, and international NGOs
- **Private Sector Growth:** 289+ registered agricultural companies across diverse value chain segments

## Conclusion

Madagascar's agricultural sector demonstrates a robust ecosystem with strong government leadership, international support, and growing private sector participation. The emergence of agritech innovations and major international partnerships signals a sector in transformation. However, opportunities exist to strengthen regional coverage, expand digital solutions, and enhance value chain integration.

The directory provides a foundation for stakeholders to identify collaboration opportunities, understand market gaps, and develop targeted interventions to support Madagascar's agricultural development goals.
