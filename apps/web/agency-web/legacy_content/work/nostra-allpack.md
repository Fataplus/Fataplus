## Dual Challenge

Designing for two different audiences simultaneously required a versatile approach. Nostra needed to seduce individual consumers with visual appeal, while AllPack needed to convince professionals with technical precision.

## Methodology

We applied a rigorous 4-phase methodology for both projects: Discovery, UX Wireframing, High-Fidelity UI, and Developer Handoff. This ensured that both platforms were not only visually stunning but also technically feasible and user-friendly.
