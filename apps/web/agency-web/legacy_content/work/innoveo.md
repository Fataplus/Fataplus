## The Challenge

Innoveo's previous digital presence did not reflect their evolving identity as a leader in CSR and Sustainability. They needed a site that was professional, easy to maintain, and cost-effective.

## The Solution

By leveraging **Figma Site**, we turned the design tool into the CMS. This allowed for a pixel-perfect translation of the brand identity without the friction of traditional coding handoffs.

## Scope

The project covered the design and implementation of 21 pages, including Home, Solutions, Portfolio, and Blog, along with a comprehensive training program for the Innoveo team.
